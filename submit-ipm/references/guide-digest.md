# Information Processing & Management (IPM): author requirements

ISSN 0306-4573 (print), 1873-5371 (online). Compiled 2026-10-30 as input for a future `submit-ipm` skill.

## How to read these notes

Tags:
- **[GUIDE]**: read in the journal's own ScienceDirect pages. Unless another URL is given, the source is the Guide for Authors (G below).
- **[ELSEVIER]**: general Elsevier policy or support page, not specific to IPM.
- **[3RD]**: third party.
- **[CHECK]**: not confirmed. Do not treat as a rule until it is seen in the portal or asked of the journal.

"Guide silent" means the full guide text was read and the item does not appear. Absence from the guide does not prove the portal will not ask for it.

Source shorthand:
- G: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/information-processing-and-management/publish/guide-for-authors

## Items a copy of the ESWA skill would get wrong

Each of these differs from the sister skill, or is not stated for IPM:
- **Preprints:** the IPM guide tells authors not to publish a preprint before the final decision (see Preprints).
- **CRediT:** required of corresponding authors in IPM, not merely encouraged.
- **Reference style:** the IPM guide does not name APA or any other style.
- **Dataset references:** in IPM, `[dataset]` goes immediately before the reference.
- **Highlights:** the IPM guide is silent; it neither requires nor encourages them.
- **Portal:** the guide's submit link goes to a new service at submit.elsevier.com/IPM, so the ESWA Editorial Manager walkthrough cannot be assumed.
