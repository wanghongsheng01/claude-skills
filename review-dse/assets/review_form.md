# Reviewer report (English)
Manuscript title. Reviewer persona.
1. Summary in your own words (3 sentences).
2. The main claim in one sentence, or "cannot state it".
3. Scores C1-C7 (1-5) with one sentence each.
4. Major issues (3-5, numbered): location, problem, required action.
5. Minor issues.
6. Sentences or passages that most harm the paper's case, quoted, with the reason.
7. What is strongest and should be promoted.
8. Recommendation: Accept / Minor / Major / Reject.
9. Confidential note to the editor.
