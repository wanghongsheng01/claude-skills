# Desk screen (English)
Hard gates (fail = returned to authors): editable source; title page separate; declarations present; blinding (manuscript, metadata, supplements); abstract 150-250; 4-6 keywords; LLM documentation; no simultaneous submission declared.

Substantive: scope area named; contribution stated in the abstract; evaluation present; references current; length justified.

Decision: send to review / desk reject / return. Reason in two sentences.
