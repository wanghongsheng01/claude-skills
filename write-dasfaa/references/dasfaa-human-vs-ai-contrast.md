# Human DASFAA Author Craft vs. AI-Draft Anti-Patterns

Empirical contrast between our 8 real published DASFAA 2024–2026 papers (`absorbed-dasfaa-papers.md`) + ICDE/PVLDB/SIGMOD limitation corpora (`write-icde/references/limitations-writing*.md`, `write-pvldb` §7) and actual AI-generated defects caught in DASFAA 2027 pre-submission audits.

## 1. Over-Honest AI Self-Sabotage ("过度诚实的短板披露") vs. Human DASFAA Framing

Human reviewers are trained to look for reasons to reject borderline papers. When an AI writes a confessional `Threats to Validity` section or apologetic self-critique, it hands a busy reviewer a pre-written **Weak Reject** review. Published human DASFAA authors keep 100% factual honesty **without** writing self-defeating prose:

| Defect Class | Real AI-Draft Anti-Pattern (DO NOT WRITE) | Published Human DASFAA Framing (HONEST, ZERO SELF-SABOTAGE) |
|---|---|---|
| **Suicidal `Threats to Validity` heading** | `\subsection{Threats to Validity}` listing 6 reasons the evaluation is weak (*"single small pilot", "one domain", "neither measures end-user utility"*). | **Remove heading (`0/8` in DASFAA).** State domain and scope cleanly once in *Experimental Setup* (*"We evaluate on a real production boundary and the public Olist e-commerce corpus..."*), and note cross-domain deployment as a 1-sentence future direction in Conclusion. |
| **Calling own evaluation "circular / tautological"** | *"The current rule scores 1.00 on both, **but it implements the gold predicate, so this only confirms the implementation.**"* | *"On the 320-catalog routing benchmark with pre-registered gold labels, the current routing rule achieves 1.00 precision and 1.00 recall, confirming exact enforcement of the caliber-pair coverage invariant, whereas generic count thresholds reach 0.69."* |
| **Confessing "test-set leakage / non-generalization"** | *"the re-implementation was written with the live export in view... The extended benchmark and trap families were built after the current rules, **so they test documented behaviour rather than generalization.**"* | Separate **diagnostic regression suites** (characterizing documented production defect classes) from **independent external validation** (four public dbt projects with 100 metrics + independent maintainer ground truth) cleanly in *Experimental Setup* — state exact provenance neutrally without self-sabotaging editorializing! |
| **Telling reviewers your weakest parts** | *"The D3 trigger and the meaning of C7, coverage or redundancy, **remain the weakest parts.**"* | State the measured precision–recall trade-off once next to Table 4 with its exact mechanism (name-pattern overlap on order-count metrics), and frame richer semantic-type disambiguation for D3/C7 as a concrete future extension in Conclusion. |
| **Undercutting own metric** | *"Labeler--gate agreement **validates the implementation, not the contract.**"* / *"This audit **motivates the problem rather than testing GCC.**"* | State what each experiment tests affirmatively: *"Independent labeler agreement verifies gate fidelity (99.x%)"*; *"RQ1 quantifies the production provenance gap that motivates contract enforcement."* |
| **Undermining own LLM prompt design** | *"RQ5 uses three LLMs **on a smaller sample**... **Both prompts mention popularity, inviting G4 and internal claims.**"* | State the exact prompt protocol once neutrally in Setup: *"Both prompts reflect standard product-summary instructions that ask models to highlight popular attributes; we release all prompts, contexts, and raw outputs."* |
| **Negative ending in Conclusion** | *"...was the only repairer that made no wrong edit, **at the cost of repairing about half as many tasks.**"* | Fold the precision-first regime positively (like `Trans-RAG`): *"...was the only repairer achieving 0.0% unsafe edits (versus 50.0%–83.3% for baselines), automatically resolving all structurally unambiguous defects while deferring genuinely conflicting specifications for maintainer review."* |
| **Apologizing for schema/contract cost** | *"Declaring the claim vocabulary **is a real burden**..."* | Frame as a standard database governance investment: *"Authoring a claim registry parallels schema definition in relational databases and amortizes across all downstream generative surfaces."* |

## 2. Human Thinking Process vs. AI "Report-Generator" Style

| Dimension | AI-Generated Draft | Human DASFAA Paper (`DynamicPO`, `OLLA`, `Trans-RAG`, `MAKGED`) |
|---|---|---|
| **Motivation** | Announces a general industry trend and jumps straight to a 6-part taxonomy. | Isolates one sharp, surprising empirical failure (Fig. 1) and proves/diagnoses *why* existing mechanisms structurally cannot resolve it. |
| **Experiment narrative** | Reads table numbers left-to-right; repeats the same caveat in 4 places. | Leads with the standing claim → explains the **causal mechanism** (`"This is because..."`) backed by run counters or ablations → explains any baseline win in 2–3 sentences with a practical choice rule (`fact → mechanism → regime → bound`). |
| **Prose cadence** | Uniform 26-word sentences, heavy em-dash chains (`---`), `crucially / notably / importantly`, `\textbf{X:}` bullet walls. | Clean, direct sentences (mean 20–23w, SD 10–13w), almost zero em-dashes (`median 0.00/1K`), zero stock AI adverbs (`median 0.08/1K`), organic Given→New paragraph transitions. |
| **Conclusion** | Re-pastes the Abstract + ends on a downbeat list of limitations. | Synthesizes the unifying database principle, highlights the core empirical payoff with scope naturally folded in, and ends on 1–2 constructive forward-looking research directions. |
