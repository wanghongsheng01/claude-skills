# PODS Craft Addendum: Aggregate View Rewriting, Query Determinacy & Authorization Views

Empirical synthesis of foundational and modern **ACM PODS / ICDT** literature on **view-based query rewriting under constraints**, **aggregate provenance semirings**, **query determinacy**, and **fine-grained authorization views** — tailored for theoretical papers on guarded aggregate rewriting and authorized ratio queries (such as `PremiseGuard`).

## 1. Canonical PODS Lineage & Mother-Tongue Coordinates
Always anchor guarded aggregate view-rewriting and authorization papers in four classical pillars from Paragraph 1 and Related Work:
1. **Aggregate Query Rewriting Using Views**: *Cohen, Nutt, and Sagiv (PODS 1999 / JACM)* — homomorphic conditions for rewriting `SUM / COUNT / AVG` group-by queries over materialized summary views, and why unguarded projection/aggregation destroys multiplicity alignment.
2. **Semiring & Semimodule Provenance for Aggregates**: *Green, Karvounarakis, and Tannen (PODS 2007)* and *Amsterdamer, Deutch, and Tannen (PODS 2011)* — why aggregates require tensor-product semimodules $K \otimes M$ where non-cancellative / non-distributive ratio combinations prevent naive predicate pushdown across numerator and denominator branches.
3. **Truman vs. Non-Truman Fine-Grained Authorization Views**: *Rizvi, Mendelzon, Sudarshan, and Roy (SIGMOD 2004)* and *Zhang and Mendelzon (ICDT 2005)* —
   - **Truman semantics** silently substitutes base relations with row-filtered authorization views $R \mapsto \sigma_{\alpha}(R)$, returning an answer to a *different* query without warning (which on ratio queries triggers unbounded relative distortion and Simpson rank reversal!).
   - **Non-Truman semantics** demands conditional equivalence $Q(D) = Q'(V_{\alpha}(D))$ across all valid instances $D \models \Sigma$, rejecting queries whose true scoped answer is not determined by authorized views. Framing safe ratio pushdown (`SafeAuth`) as **premise-relativized Non-Truman validity** immediately resonates with every PODS reviewer!
4. **Query Determinacy vs. Rewritability under Constraints**: *Nash, Segoufin, and Vianu (PODS 2007 / TODS 2010)*, *Gogacz and Marcinkowski (PODS 2015 / PODS 2016)*, and *Benedikt, ten Cate, and Tsamoura (PODS 2016)* — distinguish **information-theoretic determinacy** ($\mathcal{V}(D_1) = \mathcal{V}(D_2) \implies Q(D_1) = Q(D_2)$ for all $D_1, D_2 \models \Sigma$) from **rewritability in a target language**. Whenever proving non-rewritability of guarded ratio views, provide a **two-instance witness pair $(D_1, D_2)$** yielding identical view outputs $\mathcal{V}(D_1) = \mathcal{V}(D_2)$ yet distinct true ratios $Q(D_1) \neq Q(D_2)$: this proves **information-theoretic non-determinacy** (no rewriting in *any* language — even Turing-complete post-processing — can recover $Q$), closing a classic PODS reviewer question!
