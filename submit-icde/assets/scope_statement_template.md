# ICDE scope statement (CMT form field, at most 1000 characters)

Use only if the paper could look like pure ML/DM. Follow the three parts the CFP asks for; the CFP's examples 1-3 are accepted, "a similar paper appeared at ICDE" is not.

Template (fill, then check the length with `wc -m`):

> (a) Challenge: <the data-engineering problem, e.g. query processing cost/accuracy of operator X over relations>.
> (b) Contributions: <principled idea 1> (Sec. N), <idea 2 with guarantee> (Sec. N), <system/experiment> (Sec. N).
> (c) Builds on: <3-6 ICDE/VLDB/SIGMOD papers by short name and venue/year>.
