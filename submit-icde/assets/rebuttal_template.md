# ICDE author feedback (rebuttal) template

ICDE 2027 publishes no length limit. Draft for about 4,000 characters (an assumption) and cut to the CMT
limit when the window opens. English only. The paper cannot be revised, so cite the numbers already in
the paper or in the public artifact.

**Opening (1–2 sentences).** Thank the reviewers and state the one-line contribution they agreed on.

**Common concerns first.** Answer the points that two or more reviewers raised. Give each one a bold
label:
- **[R1-W2, R3-W1] <concern in 5 words>.** The answer, with a number and its location ("Table IV, row
  DBLP: …").
  - If the concern is about scope, restate the regime where the claim holds, with the measured
    mechanism.
  - Never concede more than the paper says.

**Per reviewer.** Use one block per reviewer and answer only the remaining questions, in their order.
- Factual misreadings: correct them politely, quoting the section or equation.
- Requests for new experiments: point to the closest existing evidence (the artifact holds the full
  logs). If a result is genuinely missing, say what it would show and why the existing results bound it.

**Closing (1 sentence).** List the clarifications you will add to the camera-ready version. Wording only:
title, abstract and authors are frozen.

Checklist:
- [ ] Every claim carries a number and a location.
- [ ] No new results that are not in the paper or artifact.
- [ ] Tone is factual and polite.
- [ ] No more than the paper's own scope is conceded.
