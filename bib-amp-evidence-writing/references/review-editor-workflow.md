# Independent review and revision

Use this division when independence is requested or the task needs an independent review. It is not a requirement to create extra agents for every copy-edit.

1. Freeze the source and compiled manuscript being reviewed, recording their hashes/version. Give the reviewer the manuscript, evidence needed to assess it and relevant exemplar analysis. Do not prewrite the review findings for that reviewer.
2. The reviewer produces findings with location, issue, evidence, scientific impact and an actionable remedy. Mark whether the issue needs wording, analysis, a source check or new data. The reviewer does not edit the manuscript they are reviewing.
3. A different editor reads the findings and revises the manuscript. Keep a response table: finding ID → accepted/partially accepted/not accepted → action and rationale → changed location. An editor may reject a requested new experiment that cannot be performed, but must scope the resulting claim accurately.
4. Return the final source, PDF and response table to the reviewer. The reviewer checks whether the remedies resolve the issues and whether edits introduced a new overclaim or inconsistency. Distinguish resolved issues from unperformed experiments or evidence that remains limited.

For the AMP evaluation setting, useful questions include:

- Does the central question have an answer in the results, rather than only a proposed reporting framework?
- Are input data, architecture, training objective, decoder, metric and assay panel distinguished?
- Does a property-summary comparison get described as a full distribution result?
- Are “independent,” “robust,” “significant,” “prospective,” and “validated” supported by the actual units and procedures?
- Does a wet-lab hit fraction use all eligible tested candidates for the stated endpoint, with strain and units defined?
- Are relevant counterresults available, including a simpler model outperforming the favored method?
- Is a cited example being used for motivation, a design choice or an actual reproduced comparison?
- Is each added reference real, supportive and cited in the final document?

The scientific conclusions are not chosen to maximize apparent acceptance. Improve clarity and prominence of strong evidence while retaining facts that materially constrain the claim. Keep author-facing quality checks out of the scientific main text when they do not contribute to the research question.
