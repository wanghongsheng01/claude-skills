Snapshot date: **2026-11-12** (America/Los_Angeles). **Re-read the live pages before every submission.** If a live page disagrees with this file, the live page wins and this file should be updated on a new branch — never by silently editing a published skill's meaning on `main` from a paper-writing session.

## Related-work placement (evidence, not a rule)

A 21-paper ICDE deep-read (already in `review-icde`) found mixed placement: LLM and theory papers more often after experiments; problem-driven and EAB papers more often after the introduction. This is a habit, not a CFP requirement. 梁's question is answered: **ICDE does not always put related work last.**
