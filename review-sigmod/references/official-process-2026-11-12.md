# SIGMOD review process — official pointers (2026-11-12 PT)

- Industrial CFP: https://2027.sigmod.org/calls_industrial_track_papers.shtml — reviews 2027-01-26, optional lightweight revision 2027-02-23, notification 2027-03-04.
- Research CFP: https://2027.sigmod.org/calls_papers_sigmod_research.shtml — double-anonymous, author feedback (revision plan + factual pointers), Accept/Reject/Minor/Major, one-month revision.
- PACMMOD reviewer guidelines (revision request examples and non-requests): https://dlnext.acm.org/journal/pacmmod/sigmod

SIGMOD is not on OpenReview. Do not invent a review form. The 2027 research track is closed; re-read that year's CFP for later cycles.
