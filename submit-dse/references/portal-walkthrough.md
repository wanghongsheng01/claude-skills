URL: https://www.editorialmanager.com/dsej. Only the welcome page was seen (2026-10-30); nothing below the login is verified. Record real screens here at first use.

Expected fields [INFERRED from Springer Editorial Manager journals]: article type, title, abstract, keywords, authors with ORCID, funding, suggested and excluded reviewers, comments, file upload by item type, declarations, build PDF, approve PDF.

Rules for the assistant:
- The author logs in, types passwords, accepts terms, chooses payment and clicks the final Submit or Approve.
- Compare the built PDF with the local one: no author names, tables and figures complete.
- Fields typed into the portal are English; instructions to the author are Chinese.
