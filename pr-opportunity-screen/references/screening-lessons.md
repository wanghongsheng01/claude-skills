# Lessons from a 2026-11-09 Pattern Recognition opportunity audit

These are published-work and source-reading lessons, not claims of experimental reproduction or editorial acceptance probability. Refresh the literature before reusing them.

1. **A new prompt wrapper is not a gap.** [TAI++ (IJCAI2024)](https://doi.org/10.24963/ijcai.2024/578) already develops text-driven multi-label adaptation. [PR hierarchical prompt tuning](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patcog.2025.112986) adds structured sharing. Compare the operation and resource boundary, not just the title.
2. **Training-free does not specify label access.** [DualPrompt (ICLR2026)](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/file/cb825b4e5d0665a339e8d1aa08d1ca7e-Paper-Conference.pdf) distinguishes language-derived and data-derived co-occurrence conditions. [SPARC (CVPR2025)](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Miller_SPARC_Score_Prompting_and_Adaptive_Fusion_for_Zero-Shot_Multi-Label_Recognition_CVPR_2025_paper.html) makes simple score/fusion controls substantive prior art. Read normalization and adaptation details before grouping these methods with a strict per-image, zero-label protocol.
3. **An anchor representation does not settle total complexity.** [Dynamic unified bipartite graph learning (PR2024)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patcog.2024.110715) still contains a sample-by-sample graph. Audit every matrix and preprocessing step; replacing one operation with a known anchor version is not automatically a new method.
4. **Protocol defects are distinct from scientific opportunities.** A teacher-update equation/prose discrepancy or unspecified model-selection split should be clarified. It does not by itself establish a new learning principle. Search adaptive teachers, meta-learning, reweighting and cross-fitting before claiming a new reliability mechanism.
5. **Watch publication dates.** A future issue, repository acceptance badge, preprint and verified formal article are different evidence states. A newer preprint can invalidate novelty without being counted as a formally accepted venue example.
6. **A convincing failure video is not a causal experiment.** Keep the observed scene and reported failure, but require synchronized raw channels and controlled nuisance changes before assigning a sensor-level cause. Record calibration/selection access and compare cheap physical adjustments as well as neural models.
7. **Hardware names are not performance evidence.** A reachable status probe, a successful baseline run, a latency measurement and an energy measurement answer different questions. Network failure does not mean hardware is absent; a model simulation does not establish deployment efficiency.

Do not turn these examples into universal dataset counts, seed counts, architectural requirements or fixed statistical thresholds.
