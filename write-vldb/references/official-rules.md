# Official PVLDB / VLDB 2027 rules (snapshot)

Checked: 2026-11-12. Re-read before submission.

- https://www.vldb.org/2027/important-dates.html
- Monthly research: abstract on the 25th of the previous month, 17:00 PT; full paper on the 1st, 17:00 PT.
- Body ≤ 12 pages including appendix and acknowledgements, excluding references.
- Single-blind. Author caps: 2 papers / author / month, 12 / year (confirm wording on the current CFP).
- Last new-paper deadline for the 2027 conference was stated as 2027-03-01 17:00 PT.
- EA&B reproducibility package at initial submission for experiment/analysis/benchmark papers.

If any official page changes, update this note and the SKILL.md numbers; do not keep a stale snapshot silently.
