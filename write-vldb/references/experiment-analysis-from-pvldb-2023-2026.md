When writing a PVLDB evaluation section:

1. Open that table and pick the two or three nearest neighbours (NL2SQL / EA&B → Dawn, DAIL-SQL, Sphinteract, TACO, SemBench, Annotation Errors, REDSQL; views/cache → Miao et al. MV pipeline; k-plex → AltRB and “Pruning Techniques Matter”; structured views → Evaporate).
2. Apply `experiment-analysis-writing` §§3–4 for first-place and second-place paragraphs.
3. Re-read https://www.vldb.org/2027/submission-guidelines.html for the 12-page body (analysis counts toward it), single-blind, author caps, EA&B package at initial submission, and related-submission declaration.
