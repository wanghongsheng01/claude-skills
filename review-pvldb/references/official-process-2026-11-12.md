# PVLDB review process — official pointers (2026-11-12 PT)

- Guidelines: https://www.vldb.org/2027/submission-guidelines.html — three reviewers, AE, revision once, desk-reject categories, 12-month embargo exceptions.
- Call: https://vldb.org/2027/call-for-research-track.html — four categories and evaluation expectations.
- EiC, Vol. 18 No. 5: https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol18/FrontMatterVol18No5.pdf — desk-rejects (format, authors on page 1, out of scope, syntactic DB citations, arXiv-heavy bibliographies).
- EiC, Vol. 19 No. 1: https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol19/FrontMatterVol19No1.pdf — SAR, at least three reviewers, three-week discussion, meta-review, Light Load Reviewers, artifact rubric, Shadow PC.
- EiC, Vol. 19 No. 3: https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol19/FrontMatterVol19No3.pdf — Self-Declaration of Related Submissions; Scope Policy.

PVLDB is not on OpenReview. Do not invent a review form.
